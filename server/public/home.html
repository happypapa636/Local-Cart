One chat, from list to doorstep

The shop stays the shop. The chat stays a chat. LocalCart adds the structure in between: a cart, a bill and an order card that both phones see change at the same moment.

  1. Type it, or hold the mic and say it. Hindi, English, or both in one sentence: “2 kilo pyaaz, 1 kg tamatar aur 1 litre doodh.”

  2. AI reads the message or voice note and matches each item to this shop’s catalog, in the right unit and at the shop’s price. Anything it can’t match stays on the cart for the shop to price.

  3. The shopkeeper ticks what’s in stock, fixes a price or a quantity, adds a delivery fee and sends the bill back into the same chat.

  4. Choose cash on delivery or UPI. The order card moves from Packed to Out for delivery to Delivered on both phones, without anyone sending a new message.

If you run a shop

  • Set up in minutes: photo, location pin, hours, delivery radius and UPI ID.
  • Paste your price list (“Aloo 30/kg, Amul Taaza 500ml 28”) and AI turns it into catalog rows to check and save.
  • New carts arrive live with an alert, and the dashboard counts what needs you.
  • A bill editor built for the counter: in stock or not, quantity, price, delivery fee, note.
  • Move the order along, confirm UPI or cash, or cancel with a reason the customer sees.
  • An open or closed switch. Closed shops still chat; they just don’t take carts.

The order lives in the chat

LocalCart runs on Nexconn Chat in the Singapore data centre. The phones use the Nexconn Android Chat SDK; our server uses the Platform Chat API to post order cards and move them along.

Why a card can’t go stale

Postgres holds the order. The server posts each card once, as a custom Nexconn message with metadata enabled. Every change after that (accepted, packed, paid, rated) is a metadata update on the same message, so both phones re-draw the card where it already is. Editing a cart uses Nexconn’s message update, so the original Cart card changes for both people.

And whenever a card scrolls into view, the app re-reads the order from the API, so even a missed realtime event can’t leave a card wrong.

Questions people ask

Which languages can I order in?

Type or speak in Hindi, English or a mix of both. Voice notes are transcribed, and the transcript is kept on the order so the shop can check what you said.

What if the shop doesn’t have something?

The shop marks it unavailable on the bill and the total updates before you accept. Items the AI couldn’t match to the catalog stay on the cart for the shop to price.

How does UPI payment work?

Tapping Pay by UPI opens your UPI app with the shop’s UPI ID and the exact amount. After paying, tap “I’ve paid”; the shop confirms when the money arrives. There’s no payment gateway in between, so LocalCart never handles your money.

Will I get alerts when the app is closed?

Not yet. Messages and order updates alert you while LocalCart is open or running in the background. Push notifications after the app is fully closed are on the list.

Who sees my phone number and address?

Only a shop you send an order to, so they can deliver it. Customers browsing shops never see each other. Read the privacy note for the details.
